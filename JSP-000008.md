# System Requirements Specification: Formal Verification of Goldbach's Conjecture Based on Reverse Symmetry and Partitioning of `1`

## 1. System Overview and Approach

This requirements specification defines the **PR submission requirements for a minimal module configuration** covering the Lean 4 formal verification (target module: `JSP-000008.lean` / namespace: `GoldbachReverseSymmetry`) and interactive visualization viewer (`JSP-000008.html`) for the binary Goldbach's Conjecture (prime-pair existence statement `GoldbachStatement` for target even number $2n$) in MathDB (Problem ID: `316061` / `JSP-000008`).

This approach centers on **reverse symmetry** under the folding map $k \mapsto 2n - k$ around $n$ (whether odd primes and other odd numbers—excluding $n$ itself—are fully reverse-symmetric), the **$Mp = Mp' - 1$ structure induced by isolating `1`** (which is neither prime nor composite), the relation $a = 2Pp + Mp'$ (including its correspondence with baseline/average structures), and the rise and interval asymmetry indicated by the quarter-square sequence **A002620 ([OEIS A002620](https://oeis.org/A002620))** ($\lfloor m^2 / 4 \rfloor = \lfloor m/2 \rfloor \lceil m/2 \rceil$). Together, these elements motivate the necessity of $Pp > 0$ and connect to the standard Goldbach statement form.

### 1.1 Key Design Points

* **Separation Between Statement and Proof Scope**: What the Goldbach statement ($\forall n \ge 2,\; \exists p, q \in \mathbb{P}\;\text{s.t.}\; p + q = 2n$) requires is the existence of a prime pair; it does not require space symmetry or specific rules for `1` to be imposed on the statement itself. Therefore, rather than restricting the domain to $3 \sim 2n - 3$ and eliminating boundary asymmetry, the proof uses the full odd space $\{1, 3, \dots, 2n-1\}$—preserving the asymmetry arising because natural numbers start at `1`—and encapsulates `1`-partitioning and reverse-symmetry breaking inside internal definitions and lemmas.

* **Relation $a = 2Pp + Mp'$, Baseline/Average Structure, and Isolation of `1` ($Mp = Mp' - 1$)**:

  * Consider the count $a$ of odd primes and the count $b$ of "other" odd elements (`1` and odd composites), with $n = a + b$ in the full odd space $\{1, 3, \dots, 2n-1\}$. Homogeneous odd-prime pairs ($Pp$) generate odd primes on both sides of the fold and therefore contribute $2Pp$, while mixed pairs ($Mp'$) between an odd prime and "other" (`1` or an odd composite) contribute to one side, yielding the exact counting identity

    $$
    a = 2Pp + Mp'
    $$

    In the viewer, reference/average lines—such as $\frac{a^2}{2(a+b)}$, $\frac{b^2}{2(a+b)}$, $\frac{a^2+b^2}{2(a+b)}$, and $\frac{ab}{a+b}$ (as well as baseline terms comparing homogeneous and mixed pair balances)—serve as reference axes for visualizing combinatorial baseline and average trends relative to this exact identity.

  * Partitioning `1` according to the properties of $2n - 1$ so that the pure mixed-pair count $Mp$ (excluding `1`) shifts to $Mp' - 1$ prevents a $Pp = 0$ equilibrium (full reverse symmetry).

| Property of $2n - 1$ | Assignment of `1` (Partner Side) | Pure Mixed Pairs $Mp$ (Excluding `1`) | Effect on Symmetry |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $2n - 1$ **is an odd prime** | Fix `1` on the "other" (non-prime) side | $(1, 2n-1)$ is excluded from mixed pairs, giving $Mp = Mp' - 1$ | Fixes asymmetry at fold endpoints, contradicting $Pp = 0$ |
| $2n - 1$ **is an odd composite** | Partition `1` toward the prime-side correspondence (asymmetric boundary) | Boundary shift yields $Mp = Mp' - 1$ for pure mixed pairs | Shifts alignment between first and second halves, contradicting $Pp = 0$ |

* **Detailed Relation to A002620 ([OEIS A002620](https://oeis.org/A002620))**:

  * The quarter-square sequence **A002620** ($0, 0, 1, 1, 2, 2, 4, 4, 6, 6, 9, 9, \dots$, with general term $\lfloor m^2 / 4 \rfloor = \lfloor m/2 \rfloor \lceil m/2 \rceil$) provides a baseline curve for partition/pair totals that rises in two-step parity increments.

  * As natural numbers start from `1` and bifurcate into primes and composites, overall fold/partition totals and rises near the center follow A002620-like step growth, while prime-distribution skew and the partitioning of `1` ($Mp = Mp' - 1$) introduce necessary asymmetry between the first half ($1 \sim n-1$) and second half ($n+1 \sim 2n-1$).

  * Because this asymmetry supports the contradiction obtained when assuming $Pp = 0$ (no homogeneous odd-prime pairs) alongside $a = 2Pp + Mp'$ and the `1`-partition shift (`mpPure + 1 = mpPrime`), it provides theoretical backing that keeps the minimal Lean skeleton (`pp_pos_of_symmetry_broken`, `exists_prime_pair_of_pp_pos`, `goldbach_from_lemmas`) coherent.

* **Exclusion of Prime $n$ Cases and Domain Separation**: Cases where $n$ is prime (such as $n = 2$ for $2n = 4$ and $n = 3$ for $2n = 6$) are excluded from the reverse-symmetry-breaking condition (`HasReverseSymmetryExcludingPrimeN`), while proof by contradiction (assuming $Pp = 0$, deriving a contradiction, and obtaining $Pp > 0$) applies to the remaining target cases. Meanwhile, `GoldbachStatement` preserves the standard $2 \le n$ target domain so that exclusion conditions do not unduly narrow the statement's scope.

## 2. Lean 4 Module Requirements (`JSP-000008.lean`)

Build cleanly with a minimal set of definitions, lemmas, and theorems, completing the connection up to `GoldbachStatement`.

```text
[GoldbachStatement / goldbach_from_lemmas] (Target Statement & Theorem Wiring)
      ▲
[exists_prime_pair_of_pp_pos] (Bridge from Pp > 0 to Prime Pair (p, q) Existence)
      ▲
[pp_pos_of_symmetry_broken] (Deriving 0 < pp via Contradiction Outside Excluded Cases)
      ▲
[HasReverseSymmetryExcludingPrimeN / PartitionOne / PairCounts] (Core Predicates, Partitioning & a = 2*Pp + Mp')
```

### 2.1 Core Definitions, Structures, Lemmas, and Theorems

* **`IsPrimeNat` / `revMap`**: Primality predicate (assuming `1` is excluded) and the reverse map $k \mapsto 2n - k$.

* **`HasReverseSymmetryExcludingPrimeN (n : ℕ)`**: Correspondence `IsPrimeNat k ↔ IsPrimeNat (revMap n k)` for $1 \le k \le n - 1$ under the condition that $n$ is not prime (excluding prime-$n$ cases such as $2n = 4, 6$).

* **`PartitionOne (n : ℕ)`**: Abstraction of partitioning `1` according to $2n - 1$ and the shift relation between pure mixed pairs $Mp$ and mixed pairs including `1` $Mp'$ (`mpPure + 1 = mpPrime`, corresponding to $Mp = Mp' - 1$).

* **`PairCounts`**: Structure tracking target/odd-prime count `a`, homogeneous odd-prime pair count `pp` ($Pp$), mixed pair count including `1` `mpPrime` ($Mp'$), and the identity `sync_identity : a = 2 * pp + mpPrime`.

* **`pp_pos_of_symmetry_broken`**: Lemma deriving `0 < pc.pp` outside prime-$n$ cases from reverse-symmetry breaking, the `1`-partition shift (`mpPure + 1 = mpPrime`), and contradiction when `pp = 0`.

* **`exists_prime_pair_of_pp_pos`**: Bridge lemma connecting `Pp > 0` conditions to the existence of a prime pair `(p, q)` summing to `2 * n`.

* **`GoldbachStatement` / `goldbach_from_lemmas`**: Target definition of the Goldbach statement and theorem deriving `GoldbachStatement` from step conditions and the bridge lemma.

### 2.2 Proof Scope for the PR Stage

* This PR scopes the type-checking and clean build of the top-level theorem `goldbach_from_lemmas : ... → GoldbachStatement` via skeleton lemmas (`pp_pos_of_symmetry_broken`, `exists_prime_pair_of_pp_pos`) and step-condition arguments (`hStep`, etc.). The symmetry implication inside `GoldbachStatement` serves as a structural placeholder at the skeleton stage to keep statement shape and lemma wiring aligned.

## 3. Interactive Viewer Specification (`JSP-000008.html`)

The verification HTML viewer bundled with the PR (`Full Odd Space Sync Viewer (Primes = a vs 1 & Odd Composites = b)`) is a real-time simulator that visualizes pair synchronization and asymmetry between odd primes ($a$) and "`1` and odd composites" ($b$) across the full odd space $\{1, 3, \dots, 2n-1\}$.

### 3.1 Control Panel and Parameter Specifications

* **`2n` (Target Even Scale)**: Sets target even number $2n$ with default `200` (even step, `min="6"`) and computes pair distributions over the full odd space $1 \sim 2n-1$ (cardinality $n = a + b$). Supports switching between small values such as `2n = 6` ($n = 3$, prime-$n$ case) and scales of `200` or higher.

* **`Y Scale` (Vertical Axis Scale Control)**: Adjusts the vertical maximum with default `30` to keep visibility clear for `Pp` onset and `Mp'` behavior near the center line $2n$. For larger `2n` where values exceed the vertical bound, `Y Scale` can be increased to inspect the full range.

* **`C-Filter` (Composite & `1` Filter)**: Switches among `1 & 3-multiples (C3)`, `1 & 3,5-multiples (C3..C5)`, `1 & 3,5,7-multiples (C3..C7)`, and `1 & All Odd Composites` to observe distribution changes as filtering progresses from small prime sieves to all odd composites while keeping `1` in non-prime group $b$.

* **`Mode` (Operation Mode)**: Switches between `Add (2 ~ 4n-2)` (sum distribution: fold around center line $2n$ / Goldbach region) and `Sub (0 ~ 2n-2)` (difference distribution: odd-spacing region).

* **`Items` (Plotted Series)**:

  * `Cp' + Pp + Mp'`: Simultaneously displays pairs of `1` and odd composites (`Cp'`), homogeneous odd-prime pairs (`Pp`), and mixed pairs of odd primes with `1`/odd composites (`Mp'`).

  * `Hp' + Mp'`: Compares homogeneous pair sum $Hp' = Cp' + Pp$ against mixed pairs (`Mp'`).

  * `Pp only (Pure Odd Prime Pairs)`: Isolates homogeneous odd-prime pairs (`Pp`) to observe $Pp > 0$ across even positions.

* **`Ref Lines` (Reference Line Display)**: Switches among `All (a, b, Averages)`, `Averages only`, and `None`, overlaying counts $a, b$ and reference lines $\frac{a^2}{2(a+b)}$, $\frac{b^2}{2(a+b)}$, $\frac{a^2+b^2}{2(a+b)}$, and $\frac{ab}{a+b}$.

### 3.2 Alignment with the Theoretical Model

* The viewer's counting definitions—placing `1` in group `b`, counting odd-prime pairs as `Pp`, and counting mixed pairs as `Mp'`—provide numerical experiments supporting the Lean relation $a = 2Pp + Mp'$ and the shift $Mp = Mp' - 1$ induced by partitioning `1`.
